Session Limits Controls:

  Three kinds of limit can stop a session: a session limit you set yourself (an AI credit cap), the
  usage limits on your account (rate limits), and your plan's budget (see copilot help billing).

Session limits:

  Session limits are opt-in. Usage accumulates across the entire session in both interactive and
  non-interactive runs, including subagents, which share the parent session's limit. /clear starts a
  fresh count and keeps the limit.

  --max-ai-credits <credits>             Cap this session (minimum 30 AI credits).
  /limits                                View or edit the current session's limit.
  /limits set max-ai-credits <credits>   Set or raise it; /limits unset removes it.

  The AI credit limit is a soft cap: usage is known only after a response returns, so one response can
  go past the limit; the next model call is then blocked. Hidden work such as compaction counts too.
  The timeline reports usage around 50%, 75%, and 90% usage ("Session limits: 15/30 AI credits used.").
  When 10% or less of the AI credit limit remains, the timeline says: "If this session continues, the
  next model call is final; tools will be disabled." When the limit is reached in interactive mode, "Session limit reached" offers Add AI credits, Set new AI credit limit, Remove
  session limit or Cancel.

Rate limits:

  Copilot also limits how much you can use over time. /usage shows them as Session and Weekly bars with
  their reset times, and Copilot warns at 50%, 75%, 90% and 95% ("You've used over 75% of your weekly
  usage limit. Your limit resets ..."). When you hit one, the message says which, and when it resets:

  "You've hit the rate limit for this model."   Switch models with /model, or wait.
  "You've hit your session rate limit."         Wait, or switch to Auto.
  "You've reached your weekly rate limit."      Wait, or switch to Auto.
  "You've hit your rate limit."                 Wait, or switch to Auto.

  Messages you queued wait automatically ("Rate limited. Pausing 2 pending messages for 5 minutes
  before continuing."), for 2 minutes if the service gave no reset time.

Switching to Auto:

  Unless you already use Auto or your own model provider, a rate limit (other than the session rate
  limit) asks "Switch to Copilot Fusion mode?" ("Switch to auto mode?" in remote sessions) with
  Yes, "Yes, and always switch when rate limited (saves a setting)" and No. The second choice turns
  on `continueOnAutoMode`, after which Copilot switches without asking; you can also set it in
  settings.json. In autopilot nothing asks, so only that setting switches models.

Examples:

  $ copilot --max-ai-credits 30
  /limits set max-ai-credits 100

  Rate limits: https://docs.github.com/copilot/concepts/rate-limits
